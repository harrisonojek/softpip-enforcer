One of the primary catalysts behind the ₦32M autopsy was the absence of account segregation. Automated algorithms and manual discretionary trades operated out of a single aggregated Deriv CR wallet. When manual CFD trades incurred floating drawdowns, the margin buffer required by automated option proposals was eliminated. When automated scripts hit a streak of losses, discretionary trades were prematurely liquidated by broker margin calls.

### The principle of physical separation

You cannot enforce risk rules if automated and discretionary capital share the same bankroll. The two domains must be physically separated at the broker account level.

In the Deriv ecosystem, you are permitted to open multiple sub-accounts under your primary identity:

1. **The Automated Governor Account (CR-Bot)**:
   - Dedicated solely to The Enforcer risk governor or sanctioned automated scripts.
   - Funded with exactly $10 (or up to $50 as you progress through milestones).
   - Zero manual trades placed via the Deriv web terminal or mobile app.
   - API tokens scoped exclusively to this account ID.
2. **The Discretionary Manual Account (CR-Manual)**:
   - Dedicated to manual chart inspection, demo practice, or MT5 CFD positions.
   - Distinct balance pool and credentials.
   - Drawdowns on this account cannot affect the margin or execution of your automated governor.

### Operational rules for account segregation

To maintain strict boundaries between accounts:

- **Never transfer funds during an active session**: If your bot account incurs a $1.00 daily loss and halts, do not transfer $1.00 from your manual account to "keep the bot running." The halt was triggered for risk containment; overriding it via internal transfers defeats the governor.
- **Isolate API token scopes**: When generating an API token for your bot, verify that the token is bound strictly to the designated bot sub-account CR number. Never use a master token that can access your primary cash balance.
- **Maintain a zero-balance reserve wallet**: Keep long-term trading profits in a separate, unlinked bank account or cold crypto wallet. Capital sitting idle in an active trading wallet creates psychological temptation to expand position sizing during emotional drawdowns.

Account segregation transforms risk management from an exercise in personal willpower into an architectural constraint enforced by software.
