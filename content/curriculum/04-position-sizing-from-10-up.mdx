Professional risk management literature recommends risking no more than 1% to 2% of total equity on any single trading idea. In conventional equity or forex markets with fractional micro-lots, an account with $5,000 capital can easily risk 1% ($50) with appropriate stop-loss placement.

When you fund a $10 test account on Deriv, that conventional guidance breaks down immediately against hard broker constraints.

### The $0.35 broker floor constraint

On Deriv synthetic index Options contracts (specifically Volatility 75 Rise/Fall), the broker enforces a hard minimum stake floor of $0.35 per contract. You cannot stake $0.10, $0.05, or $0.01.

Calculate the percentage of your capital risked on trade one:

```
Risk per Trade = $0.35 / $10.00 = 3.50%
```

On your very first trade, you are forced to risk 3.50% of your entire capital base. If you experience two consecutive losses, your balance drops to $9.30, and your third $0.35 stake now represents:

```
Adjusted Risk = $0.35 / $9.30 ≈ 3.76%
```

At a 3.5% initial risk floor:
- 10 consecutive losses reduce your account to $6.50 (a 35% drawdown).
- 20 consecutive losses reduce your account to $3.00 (a 70% drawdown).
- 28 consecutive losses completely exhaust your equity down to $0.20, at which point the broker rejects the next contract due to `InsufficientBalance`.

### Operating within the constraint

Because you cannot reduce your stake below <SpecVal k="base_stake_usd" />, you must adjust your operational framework:

1. **Flat stake discipline**: Keep the stake at <SpecVal k="base_stake_usd" /> (`sizing_mode: fixed`). Do not switch to `percent` sizing until equity crosses $100 where 1% exceeds the <SpecVal k="base_stake_usd" /> floor.
2. **Cool-off & daily loss limit (<SpecVal k="daily_loss_limit_floor_usd" /> floor / <SpecVal k="daily_loss_limit_pct" /> equity)**: After <SpecVal k="max_consecutive_losses" /> consecutive losses (<SpecVal k="pre_cooloff_ladder_risk_usd" /> cumulative risk), the governor triggers a mandatory <SpecVal k="cool_off_minutes" />-minute cool-off and resets the ladder to step 1 before the daily cap of max(<SpecVal k="daily_loss_limit_floor_usd" />, <SpecVal k="daily_loss_limit_pct" />) fires. Your hard account preservation stop remains active above <SpecVal k="balance_floor_usd" />.
3. **Session duration cap (<SpecVal k="max_session_trades" /> trades)**: Do not allow a bot to run indefinitely. The longer an account trades at a 3.5% risk floor, the higher the mathematical certainty of encountering an extended losing streak. Enforce a hard <SpecVal k="max_session_trades" />-trade cap per session.

Accepting that <SpecVal k="base_stake_usd" /> is 3.5% of your account is the first step toward disciplined risk governance.
