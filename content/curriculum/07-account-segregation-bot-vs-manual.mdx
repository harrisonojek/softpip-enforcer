One of the primary structural catalysts behind the ₦32M autopsy was the absence of account segregation. Automated execution scripts and manual discretionary trades operated out of a single aggregated Deriv CR wallet pool. When manual CFD trades on MT5 incurred floating drawdowns, the margin buffer required by automated option proposals was eliminated. When automated scripts experienced a standard drawdown cluster, discretionary trades were prematurely liquidated by broker margin calls.

You cannot enforce systematic risk rules if automated and discretionary capital share the same bankroll. The two domains must be physically separated at the broker account level.

### The principle of physical separation

Discretionary human trading and automated algorithmic execution operate on diametrically opposed psychological and operational axes:

1. **Human Discretionary Trading**:
   - High cognitive load and emotional susceptibility.
   - Variable holding periods and flexible stop targets.
   - Vulnerable to revenge trading, hesitation, and impulsive sizing escalation.
2. **Automated Risk Governor Execution**:
   - Zero cognitive fatigue and deterministic execution speed.
   - Fixed mathematical sizing, rigid cool-off timers, and process exits.
   - Completely vulnerable to software exceptions, network disconnects, and unmonitored script loops.

When both modes share one wallet, the vulnerabilities of both domains compound. A bad manual trade causes the human to tamper with the bot's risk parameters to recover capital faster. An automated losing streak provokes the human to manually intervene with oversized discretionary bets.

### Implementing CR segregation on Deriv

In the Deriv ecosystem, you are permitted to open multiple sub-accounts under your primary verified profile. You must configure two distinct accounts:

1. **The Automated Governor Account (CR-Bot)**:
   - Dedicated solely to The Enforcer risk governor or sanctioned automated scripts.
   - Funded with exactly $10 (or up to $50 as you progress through milestone levels).
   - Zero manual trades placed via the Deriv web terminal, DTrader, or mobile app.
   - API tokens scoped exclusively to this specific CR sub-account number with `Read` and `Trade` permissions only.
2. **The Discretionary Manual Account (CR-Manual)**:
   - Dedicated to manual chart inspection, demo practice, or MT5 CFD positions.
   - Distinct balance pool and independent credentials.
   - Floating drawdowns on this account have zero mathematical access to the margin of your automated bot.

### Three non-negotiable operational rules

To preserve strict isolation between these accounts, enforce three mechanical boundaries:

1. **Never transfer funds during an active session**: If your bot account incurs a $1.00 daily loss and hits its circuit breaker, you will feel an immediate urge to transfer $1.00 from your manual wallet to "keep the session going." You must recognize that the halt was triggered to protect capital from adverse market variance. Overriding it via internal transfers defeats the governor.
2. **Strictly scope API tokens**: When generating API credentials on `developers.deriv.com`, verify that the token is bound exclusively to the designated bot sub-account. Never generate a master token from an account that holds substantial cash reserves. If a script encounters an infinite loop or unhandled exception, your total financial exposure is physically capped at the $10 balance in that isolated CR wallet.
3. **Maintain a zero-balance reserve wallet**: Keep reserve trading capital in a separate, unlinked bank account or cold crypto wallet. Capital sitting idle in an active trading wallet creates continuous psychological pressure to increase position sizing during drawdowns.

Account segregation transforms risk management from a fragile exercise in human willpower into an architectural boundary enforced by software.
