Losing weeks and losing months are a standard statistical feature of any non-fraudulent trading system. If a promoter claims their system delivers uninterrupted green months 12 months a year, they are either fabricating records or executing an unhedged Martingale doubling strategy that has not yet encountered its terminal liquidation streak.

The objective of a risk governor is not to eliminate losing months; it is to ensure that a losing month consumes a strictly bounded fraction of capital rather than your entire account. When a month closes in the red, you do not abandon the framework or tweak indicators on emotional impulse. You execute a structured, week-by-week technical audit.

### Week 1: Baseline Variance & Circuit Breaker Verification

The first week of an adverse month typically begins with a normal clustering of losing trades. Your audit for Week 1 focuses entirely on mechanical safety:

1. **Verify Circuit Breaker Activation**: Inspect `journal.db` to confirm that the governor halted execution every time the daily loss threshold ($1.00 on a $10 account) was approached. Did any day incur a loss greater than $1.00? If yes, identify why an order was dispatched beyond the limit.
2. **Audit Streak Dynamics**: Identify your longest consecutive loss streak during the week. If your worst streak was 3 or 4 losses, the 1.3× ladder operated within expected statistical tolerances.
3. **Verify Cool-Off Disconnects**: Confirm that after three consecutive losses, the socket dropped for the full 900 seconds (`cool_off_seconds: 900`). Verify that zero manual proposals were submitted during the enforced cooling intervals.

### Week 2: Psychological Compliance & Sizing Drift Analysis

The second week of a drawdown is where human emotional breakdown usually occurs. Traders become frustrated by the initial deficit and attempt subtle rule circumventions:

1. **Calculate the Compliance Ratio**: Divide the count of trades matching the exact prescribed stake ($0.35 or calculated ladder step) by the total number of executed trades:
   ```
   Compliance Ratio = Compliant Trades / Total Trades
   ```
   This metric must be exactly 100%. If the ratio is 95%, you manually overrode the governor on five trades, injecting uncontrolled emotional variance into the sample.
2. **Audit Sizing Escalation**: Review whether any single contract stake exceeded $1.00 (the Step 5 ceiling of the 1.3× ladder). If an order was placed at $1.50 or $2.00, document the sizing drift as an execution violation.
3. **Session Duration Check**: Confirm that no continuous trading session exceeded the 60-minute hard kill switch (`session_timeout_seconds: 3600`). Extended screen time during drawdowns degrades pattern recognition and triggers fatigue.

### Week 3: Regime Diagnostic & Dealer Friction Calculation

In Week 3 of an adverse month, you audit whether market microstructure or broker conditions shifted:

1. **Payout Rate Inspection**: Check the recorded payout percentages across all executed contracts in your journal. Did Deriv lower the Rise/Fall payout from 92% to 84% during your trading hours? A 92% payout requires a 52.1% breakeven win rate; an 84% payout elevates the required breakeven win rate to 54.3%.
2. **Calculate Total Dealer Friction**: Multiply total executed contracts by the dealer edge ($0.0175 per $0.35 contract at 90% payout). If you executed 80 contracts over the week, you surrendered approximately $1.40 purely to dealer spread friction.
3. **Volatility Regime Mapping**: Verify whether the underlying index (e.g., Volatility 75) experienced extreme compression or expansion that violated your entry strategy's directional momentum assumptions.

### Week 4: Capital Reconciliation & System Adaptation Protocol

At the close of Week 4, you aggregate the month's metrics to make an evidence-based capital decision:

1. **Peak-to-Trough Drawdown Reconciliation**: Calculate the maximum account drawdown across the full 30-day window:
   ```
   Max Drawdown = (Peak Equity - Lowest Trough Equity) / Peak Equity × 100%
   ```
   On a $10 account managed by The Enforcer, maximum monthly drawdown across an adverse regime should not exceed $2.50 to $3.50 (25% to 35%). If your account survived with $7.00 remaining, your risk governance successfully prevented ruin during a hostile regime.
2. **Rule Adherence vs Luck Diagnosis**:
   - A red month where every rule, cool-off, and ladder cap was respected is an **engineering success**. You survived an adverse variance cluster with capital intact.
   - A green month where rules were violated, stop limits removed, or stakes doubled is an **imminent catastrophe**.
3. **The Actionable Verdict**: If your compliance ratio was 100% and your capital survived, make zero changes to sizing. If your compliance ratio fell below 100%, step back to demo execution until 200 consecutive rule-compliant trades are logged.
