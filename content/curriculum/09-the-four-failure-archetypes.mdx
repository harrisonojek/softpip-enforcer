After analyzing hundreds of liquidated accounts and reviewing the ₦32M autopsy, account blowups consistently resolve into four distinct failure archetypes. They are not random misfortunes; they are recurring behavioral and structural traps that repeat with predictable precision. To survive on a $10 account, you must diagnose which archetype governs your impulses and neutralize it through automated constraints.

### 1. Revenge Trading

Revenge trading is the acute emotional reaction to an unexpected loss streak. When a trader experiences three or four consecutive contract losses, the brain perceives the financial deficit as a personal slight rather than a standard variance event.

- **The Trigger**: A rapid drawdown cluster occurring within a narrow 10-minute window.
- **The Behavioral Loop**: Cortisol spikes and rationality degrades. The trader switches from patient analysis to frantic execution, increasing contract frequency and elevating stake sizes from $0.35 to $1.50 or $3.00 to "force the market to give back the money" before the session ends.
- **The Mathematical Reality**: Submitting random, oversized orders into an adverse variance regime dramatically amplifies the probability of complete account liquidation. A series of two additional losses at elevated stakes eliminates the entire $10 balance.
- **The Enforcer Neutralization**: The governor enforces an automated 15-minute socket disconnect (`cool_off_seconds: 900`) immediately upon recording three consecutive losses. The connection drops, locking you out of order dispatch until neurochemical equilibrium is restored.

### 2. Martingale Addiction

Martingale addiction is the refusal to accept a localized loss, substituting exponential sizing escalation for analytical discipline. It is rooted in the gambler's fallacy: the mistaken belief that because a loss has occurred multiple times, a win is "due" on the subsequent trade.

- **The Trigger**: Experiencing consecutive losses and feeling an intolerance for booking a red trade on the daily statement.
- **The Behavioral Loop**: Doubling contract stakes after every loss ($0.35 → $0.70 → $1.40 → $2.80 → $5.60).
- **The Mathematical Reality**: In a binary options environment with fixed payouts (e.g. 90%), geometric doubling requires $10.85 in cumulative risk across just 5 steps. A $10 bankroll cannot even complete step 5; it is liquidated on the fifth trade. In any sample of 200 trades, streaks of 6 to 8 consecutive adverse ticks occur with certainty.
- **The Enforcer Neutralization**: The governor enforces a linear 1.3× ladder strictly capped at 5 steps, limiting total cumulative progression exposure to $3.17 before executing a mandatory hard reset to the base $0.35 floor.

### 3. News Chasing

News chasing is the attempt to trade synthetic index derivatives around macroeconomic events, central bank announcements, or social media rumors.

- **The Trigger**: High-impact headlines (US Non-Farm Payrolls, CPI releases, interest rate decisions) or viral trading screenshots posted in Telegram groups.
- **The Behavioral Loop**: Traders leave their calibrated synthetic index charts to place impulsive, high-leverage positions on currency pairs or commodities during extreme volatility releases, or mistakenly assume that real-world economic news impacts Deriv synthetic index feeds.
- **The Mathematical Reality**: Deriv synthetic indices are generated by cryptographic pseudo-random number generators (PRNGs) with constant volatility parameters. Real-world geopolitical events have zero correlation with Volatility 75 tick generation. Chasing news on synthetic indices trades pure noise, while chasing news on live FX with a $10 balance results in instant liquidation via spread expansion.
- **The Enforcer Neutralization**: The Enforcer restricts order submission strictly to designated synthetic symbols (`R_10`, `R_75`, `R_100`) and evaluates fixed tick expirations, completely bypassing external macroeconomic feeds.

### 4. Backtest Romanticism

Backtest romanticism is the belief that an equity curve curve-fitted over historical tick data guarantees future yield in live market conditions.

- **The Trigger**: Running a simulation script across 10,000 historical ticks and observing a simulated win rate of 78% or a hypothetical 10× return.
- **The Behavioral Loop**: The engineer falls in love with the model, confusing historical curve-fitting with structural edge. When deployed live, real-world execution latency, slippage, and regime shifts produce drawdowns that the backtest never anticipated. The trader disables risk governors, believing the live market is "wrong" and will soon revert to the backtest curve.
- **The Mathematical Reality**: Past ticks in a PRNG contain zero predictive memory of future ticks. An indicator optimized on historical data merely memorizes past noise. When live variance deviates from the backtest, traders who romanticized their simulation hold losing positions into total ruin.
